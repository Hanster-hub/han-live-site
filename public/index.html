About

I'm an Applied Robotics and AI Engineer based in Singapore, with a PhD in Engineering Product Development from the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD). I build intelligent systems from algorithm design and model development through integration, testing, and real-world deployment.

As an Applied AI Engineer (Team Lead) at Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT), I lead development of industry-focused AI solutions across AI/ML, user interfaces, data storage, and cloud deployment. My work includes LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agentic workflows, and human-in-the-loop PPE violation detection using LLM-based visual reasoning.

My robotics research background spans perception, localisation, and multi-sensor fusion. At SUTD, I combined UWB, LiDAR, and odometry for multi-robot localisation, developed machine learning methods for NLOS mitigation, and collaborated with DSO on autonomous search-and-rescue applications.
